# Brief template (fill every section; the agent stops and asks if a required one is empty)

A packshot image of the product is required alongside it.

```
# <Brand> - <Product> brief
Client / brand:            (required)
Offer:                     product name, size, price or offer text (required)
Channel and audience:      placements (9:16, 4:5), age gate, who it is for (required)
Packshot image:            path to the real pack image with legible label (required)

## Product facts           only facts the agent may say or show (required)
- ingredients/forms, doses, certifications as printed on the label

## Angles the facts support (required, 2-4)
## Proof we may use (required)
## Offer / CTA options     (required, at least 3: they become the 3 closes)
## Must not (claim boundaries) (required)
- disease claims, guarantees of results, "doctor recommended", testimonial outcomes as clinical results, required disclaimer text

## Creative constraints (optional)
- creator look/age/setting if fixed (default: H3 invents), bad-product beat (default: pill pile, no competitor bottle),
  cutaways (default: muted packshot for any readable label text), end-card text (AI-creator disclosure, disclaimers)
- hook places (default: one place per hook, e.g. car / bathroom / kitchen; the body and closes share one place)
- b-roll (default: first-person library clips per references/broll.md: pain, failed, discovery, product_use, lifestyle, after, xray)

## Run settings (optional, defaults in brackets)
takes per ad [3 = 45 s; 4 = 60 s], draft or HQ [draft sample first, HQ for finals], spend cap USD [ask user]
```
