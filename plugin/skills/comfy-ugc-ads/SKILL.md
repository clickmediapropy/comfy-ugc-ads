# UGC ads on Comfy Cloud

Input: a filled brief (`references/brief-template.md`) + a packshot image. Output: final MP4s (9:16 and 4:5), a QA report with timestamps, a manifest with the cost ledger. Details live in `references/`; scripts in `scripts/` (all take paths/args, none hardcode a brand).

Hard rules: never print, log or store credentials (env vars and `comfy login` only). No paid call without the quote + explicit user OK. Ledger every paid attempt, failures included. Stay inside the brief's Must-not list. Do not claim the output replaces an editor.

Work dir: `runs/<brand>-<date>/` (briefs, scripts, prompts, takes, finals, `jobs.jsonl`, `ledger.json`). Below, `$W` is that dir.

Where this runs: run every command from this skill's folder (the one holding this SKILL.md). With no `comfy` CLI (claude.ai chat, Cowork), use web mode, `references/web-mode.md`. Its plan B installs the CLI in the code sandbox (`pip install comfy-cli`, OAuth sign-in) so HeyGen and file uploads run as on a computer. Web mode itself is the same steps through the Comfy Cloud connector's tools, with `scripts/web_overrides.py` in place of `take_graph.py --submit`.

## 1. Preflight
`python3 scripts/preflight.py`. If anything required is MISSING, tell the user exactly which item and how to provide it (env var or `comfy login`), then stop. The turbo LoRA of voice/product takes (LightX2V `minimax_h3_ref2v_turbo_8step_v1.0_768p`) is a user-imported model, not in Comfy's public catalog: on a shared Comfy Cloud Team workspace it is already there once anyone imported it; otherwise preflight prints how to import it (Models > Import, Creator plan or above), and until then those takes run without `--turbo`. Never ask them to paste a secret into chat. Also confirm the license note in `references/h3-comfy-cloud.md` (H3 community license excludes US/EU/UK/KR use) has been shown to the user.

## 2. Brief
Read the brief and packshot. Every required section must be filled (template). If not, ask, in one grouped message (max 3 questions). Extract: product facts, angles, 3 CTA options, Must-not list, settings (takes per ad default 3 = 45 s, cap).

## 3. Script (3 x 1 x 3)
Write `$W/script.md`:
- 3 hooks (different angle each, about 10-20 s), 1 body (shared by all ads), 3 closes (one CTA each). 9 ads = hook i + body + close j. Each part is one or more takes: speech takes (4-15 s each) plus the action takes cut in between them. Only the parts are rendered, not the 9 ads (a 3x1x3 with an action in every hook is about 14 takes).
- Product rule: the product never appears in the pain part (a negative-tone take or its stills). On screen while the creator describes the problem, it reads as the product that failed. The body opens on a cut with the product already in hand: the reveal. Lint `PRODUCT_IN_PAIN`; `review.py --still` flags a product left in a still.
- Seam rule: takes meet on a shared keyframe still (step 4), except at the reveal cut. All takes of a hook start and end on one hook still, so an action inside a hook puts each prop back (shake the pill jar and set it down: a poured pill pile would vanish at the next seam). The body's last still starts every close (`--guide`). A lasting change ends its take on a new still; every later take starts from it and describes that first frame with its own `props`/`hands` in the spec. Hook takes stop before the body's opening line (prevents "said twice").
- Staging rule: every object a take moves is visible in its first frame and within reach of the free hand (a glass on the island in front of him, not in another room). Declare it in `spec.props`. The phone hand is the camera: beats move the free hand only (lint `CAMERA_HAND`).
- Places and camera: one place per hook, a different one for each (car, bathroom, kitchen: three hooks that look like three real moments), and one place for the body and all closes (the closes start on the body's last still). The place changes only at the hook-to-body reveal cut. A hook take sets its own `setting`, `camera`, `camera_where`, `props`, `hands`; tag every take with `part` (`hook1`..`hook3`, `body`, `close1`..`close3`) and lint `SCENE_SPLIT` holds the rule. `camera`: `selfie` (default, arm's length front camera), `propped` (the phone stands on `camera_where`, e.g. the dashboard: a steady frame and both hands free; `hands` then says where both hands are, lint `CAMERA_MISMATCH`), `pov` (first-person b-roll only). The same creator and voice in every place: the hook still is made from the body's base still (step 4) and hook speech takes take the voice reference.
- Performance: give a line its delivery when it has one (`"delivery": "in a low, confiding tone"`, `"with a short amused huff"`) and the take its face (`"face": "warm and wry"`); the compiler adds the phone-camera realism (micro-jitters, autofocus, unretouched skin), tiny breaths in the voice and the iPhone microphone sound by itself.
- Natural speech: real creators say "um", "like,", "you know" and trail off ("so..."). Script a filler where a person would really pause (one or two per take, never in the CTA line); "..." is timed as a short pause. Never put directions in a line: "(laughs)" gets spoken (lint `SAY_DIRECTION`); a laugh is an action take.
- B-roll plan: for each hook and the body, mark 1-3 lines a first-person b-roll clip can illustrate (the pain, a failed fix, the product in use, life after) with its category; `references/broll.md`. Pain and failed b-roll never shows the product. Reuse library clips first; only missing shots are rendered.
- Two kinds of take. **speech**: lines plus small gestures. **action**: one physical action with no line (drinking, shaking a pill jar, picking up the bottle). Any non-verbal sound the script wants (yawn, gulp, sigh, laugh) gets its own action take, cut into the speech take in assembly; never inside a speech take. Never ask H3 or HeyGen for an impact sound (pills dropping into a bin, a glass clinking on marble, a thud): they render impacts badly, and no sound beats a bad one. Soft sounds in place are fine and help (a faint rattle of the pills in her palm, the cardigan's fabric rustle, a fridge hum): lint `SOUND_EFFECT` refuses only impacts. Small objects (pills, capsules, gummies, powder) never fall on screen: they break up mid-air and the model adds a drop sound anyway; keep them in a closed fist or a container and let the drop happen out of frame (lint `SMALL_FALL`). After an action the hand goes back to rest; nobody shows the result to the lens, such as an empty palm (lint `SHOW_RESULT`). Picking up, putting down or pouring is an action take too, and the speech take after it starts from the still where it is done (lint `FAST_ACTION` refuses a moving object at turbo).
- Write actions as physical steps: reach and grip, path, use, put down, release ("his fingers and thumb close firmly around the glass", "he lifts it to his mouth until the rim rests on his lower lip"). Write only what is seen and heard: no negations ("no smile", "nothing floats" come back as smiles and floating props). H3 reads every word as content: `references/failure-classes.md`.
- Bad-magnesium (or "bad alternative") beat: a plain unlabeled pill jar or tub (inside a hook it is shaken and set back down, per the seam rule), never a branded competitor bottle (unless the user supplies one and signs off in writing).
- Our label text: wherever the label must be readable, cut away to the real packshot as a muted cutaway (the packshot image itself, laid in by ffmpeg, never H3-generated text). H3 only shows the bottle in passing.
- Expression follows the emotion of the scene: a pain-point take (insomnia, fog, stomach pain) is `"tone": "negative"` (tired, serious face and closing state; the lint refuses any smile in it); smiles only where the tone turns positive.
- Claim check every line against Must-not before the quote. Put the AI-creator disclosure/disclaimer text on the end card if the brief says so.
Also write `$W/spec.json` (shape and example: `references/take-spec.example.json`: brand-neutral creator, voice, setting, camera, props, hands, still `details` and `light`; per take its part, kind, tone, timed beats, soundscape and end state, plus its own place when it is a hook in another place; `"seconds": "auto"` by default). `take_graph.py` compiles each take's H3 prompt (official MiniMax format, `scripts/h3prompt.py`) and graph from it; nothing is hardcoded to a brand. Free checks per take: `python3 scripts/take_graph.py --spec $W/spec.json --take N --guide <still> --end-guide <still> --lint` (blocks the patterns behind past defects; fix the spec, do not waive a rule without telling the user) and `--print-prompt`.
Show the script to the user only if the brief asks for approval; otherwise it is part of the quote.

## 4. Keyframe stills
Every take starts on a still (and on the H3 fallback also ends on one; without a still the model drifts to other people and places). Write every still prompt with `python3 scripts/take_graph.py --spec $W/spec.json --take N --print-still [--still-base M] [--still-end]` (free): the iPhone UGC formula of `references/stills.md` (capture type, person and expression, props and hands, framing, the place's small real details `details`, practical `light`, the anti-gloss finish), so the creator and the room pass as a real phone photo. Make one base still per scene with the Comfy partner node `openai/images-generations` (`params: {model: "gpt-image-2.5-sunburst", size: "1024x1536", quality: "high"}`; #1 for text-to-image and for image editing on the Artificial Analysis leaderboard, 2026-09-30; if `partner_generate` answers `cannot select model`, run the same call as a workflow with the `OpenAIGPTImageNodeV2` node): vertical selfie of the creator in the setting, every prop of the scene visible and within reach, hands as in `spec.hands`, and the product nowhere in the hook still (product rule). Every other still of the same person (a different pose, the bottle in hand, the last frame of a take) is an edit of the base (`medias: [{role: "image", prompt_id: <base>}]`) so the face and room stay identical. Crop each with `python3 scripts/media.py fitframe <in.png> <out.png> --w=864 --h=1536` (the HeyGen default; 576x1024 for an H3 draft). A hook in another place is a new photo of the same person: `--still-base <body take>` and the body's base still as the first image. Gate each still: `python3 scripts/review.py <still.png> --still --spec $W/spec.json --take N [--same-as <base still>]` (N = a take that starts from that still; exit 1 = a prop is missing or out of reach, the hands or camera differ, it looks AI or staged (`realism_issues`), or with `--same-as` it is not the same person: regenerate the still).

## 5. Quote, explicit OK
Quote: takes x price per take (the default model, HeyGen Video 1 at 768p: $0.32 per 15 s, 5 s minimum, billed 2026-10-01; the H3 fallback at HQ: a 15 s speech take is $0.44 at 8 turbo steps, an action insert about $0.45 at 30 steps), plus the keyframe stills (one gpt-image-2 call each; Comfy shows the price), plus review cost (3 Gemini calls per clip, measured about $0.01 per 15 s clip at assumed list prices; see `cost_usd_est` in the review JSON), plus one sample take, plus the b-roll shots the library is missing (one 4-5 s action take each at base 25 steps, about $0.10 draft, plus its still; a shot that fails twice on H3 is quoted again on a partner model, Kling or Seedance, at its own price). Give a total and a cap. Wait for an explicit "yes" to that number. Record the OK and cap in `$W/manifest.json`. No OK, no paid call.

## 6. Sample take first
Realism pass before every paid render: print the prompt (`heygen_take.py --spec $W/spec.json --take N --print-prompt`; for H3, `take_graph.py --print-prompt`) and read it with one goal, "make it look and sound even more like a real phone video". Ask: would a real person do this (no showing the result to the lens, no pills falling); does each line have a delivery (breath, tone, a huff); are the camera and skin imperfect; are the sounds the room's own; is every detail concrete ("a faint fridge hum", not "cinematic" or "beautiful")? Fix it in the spec (`delivery`, `face`, staging, soundscape), not by hand-editing the prompt, so every take gets it. The compiler and lint stop known defects; they do not make a clip feel real. (2026-10-01: Cowork, told only "improve the script and realism, make it look even more real", rewrote our own workflow's prompt and beat every take we had compiled.)
**Model: HeyGen Video 1 is the default** (Nico, 2026-10-01, until he says otherwise; `h3prompt.DEFAULT_MODEL`): in his side-by-side of the same clip it looked better, cost $0.32 instead of $0.45 per 15 s and took about 1.3 minutes instead of 6 (`references/heygen.md`). HeyGen sees only the first still: the image-to-video node has no last frame, no voice reference and no product reference (the reference-to-video node, `HeyGenReferenceToVideoNode`, accepts `@Audio1` voice references: use it to hold a voice across clips once tested). So on that node the product must be in the still (the reference-to-video node takes several reference images, so the packshot can go in as a second one, `@Image2`, named as the product in the prompt; untested), each take ends free (keep `end_state` simple: the hand at rest, eyes on the lens; the cut to the next part is a jump cut), and the voice is held by the same `voice` text and the same seed (the spec seed) for every take of a creator. **H3 is the fallback**: when a creator's voice changes between clips (listen in step 8), re-render that creator's later speech takes with H3, using take 1's audio as the voice reference (`take_graph.py ... --voice-take <take 1>`), as below.
Render ONE take first (take 1, the speaker's first speech take), only after the user's explicit OK to the quote:
`python3 scripts/heygen_take.py --spec $W/spec.json --take N --still <first still> --work $W --user-ok --cap <cap_usd>`
It compiles the prompt from the spec (natural language, timed beats, the audio last), runs the lint and the prompt check (`--check` is free), the spend guard, records the job and downloads `$W/takes/<name>-T<N>-heygen-768p-<try>.mp4`. The rest of this step is the H3 fallback.
H3 fallback: render with `scripts/take_graph.py` (every later speech take uses take 1's audio as the voice reference) (it builds the graph, so do not hand-write one; `references/h3-comfy-cloud.md` documents what it builds). Always run the free checks first: `take_graph.py --spec $W/spec.json --take N --quality draft|hq --work $W --validate` (checks every node, input and model choice against Comfy Cloud). Then submit, only after the user's explicit OK to the quote:
`python3 scripts/take_graph.py --spec $W/spec.json --take N --guide <first still> --end-guide <last still> --work $W --submit --user-ok --cap <cap_usd> [--est <usd>]`
The default is the approved setting (Nico, 2026-10-01, Cowork take af42f2a7): HQ 768x1376, dense attention, int8 text encoder, and for speech takes the 8-step turbo LoRA (`--no-turbo` for base steps, `--quality draft` for a cheap 576x1024 test). Action takes run the base model at 30 steps automatically. Submit runs the lint first and refuses on errors.
It runs the spend guard (`ledger.py check` logic) before anything is uploaded, refuses without `--user-ok` or `--cap`, records the job id in `$W/jobs.jsonl` right after submit, waits, and downloads to `$W/takes/<name>-T<N>-<quality>-<try>.mp4`. On a timeout or crash, resume with `--collect <prompt_id>`; never resubmit blindly (check `comfy --json jobs status <id> --where cloud`). Review the take (section 8). Show the user the take and its QA result; if it fails the rubric after the fix loop, fix the prompt and ask again before spending more. Draft vs HQ decision is made here: a different setting means a different woman, so re-render the whole chain.

## 7. Batch
Render the remaining takes with the same `heygen_take.py` call (or `take_graph.py --submit` on the H3 fallback), one job per take (in parallel up to the workspace limit; each call guards and records its own job). Every take gets its first still (`--still`; on H3 `--guide` and `--end-guide`). On H3, speech takes after a speaker's first take pass `--voice-take <that take.mp4>` (voice reference); action takes never take a voice. Use a distinct `--try` suffix per attempt. Hook takes start and end on the hook still (seam rule). Before a resume, check which jobs are still in flight. Stop on budget. B-roll shots render the same way from the library's own spec and work dir (`runs/broll-<brand>-<who>/`); each clip that passes review goes into the index with `python3 scripts/broll.py add` (`references/broll.md`).

## 8. Review + bounded fix loop (every take, then every assembled ad)
Review: `python3 scripts/review.py <clip.mp4> --spec $W/spec.json --take N --out $W/reviews/<name>.json` runs the rubric: frame (`cropcheck`), transcript diff against the spec's lines (an action take must have no speech at all; its scripted yawn or gulp is not a defect), visual candidates, a props check (an object floating, popping in or leaving the hand), hair check on head crops. Output JSON: `score` (0 = clean), timestamped `findings`, `fix` hint. It costs a few cents of Gemini per clip (tokens and cost estimate are in the JSON). It finds candidates only; also look at `media.py strip` and treat clean as "no defects found". With HeyGen, also listen to every speech take of a creator one after another: a different voice is a defect (no voice reference), fixed with the H3 fallback (step 6).
Fix: `python3 scripts/fix.py <clip.mp4> --out <fixed.mp4> --spec $W/spec.json --take N --review <review.json> --report <fix.json>` applies the bounded loop: max 2 attempts, free ffmpeg cuts of removable ranges (`media.py cut`), a fix is kept only if its re-review score is strictly lower. Exit 0 = clean/kept, 2 = NEEDS EDITOR (original is copied to `--out`, timestamps in the report), 3 = the policy needs a re-render. A re-render is paid and done by you: new `--try` (and on H3 a new `--seed`) with `heygen_take.py` or `take_graph.py --submit` (guard + ledger as usual), a positive one-line feedback sentence in the take's prompt, then `fix.py ... --rerender-candidate <new.mp4>`, which keeps it only if its score is strictly lower.

## 9. Assemble and export
**On Comfy Cloud (the default, fast):** write `$W/plan.json` (the cuts per part, the packshot cutaways, the end card; format in the script's help) and run `python3 scripts/assemble_graph.py --plan $W/plan.json --work $W --validate` (free), then `--submit --user-ok --cap <usd>`. One Cloud job per ad renders its 9:16 and 4:5 (a 66 s ad ran in 23 s of Cloud time on 2026-10-01, about 1.7 minutes end to end with the uploads; local ffmpeg on the Mac took about 1.3 minutes per ad, one after another), at the takes' native size. It levels each take's loudness, cuts on whole frames so picture and sound stay in sync, keeps the voice under the packshot cutaway, and fits the packshot to each format. The `media.py` steps below are the local fallback (and the way to 1080x1920, since Cloud has no video-scale node).
A sound the model added in a silent window (a thud when a fist leaves the frame, a clink) is not fixed by a re-render: replace the take's audio from just after the last word with its own room tone, `media.py roomtone <take> <out> <t0>` (the picture is untouched).
Action takes are rendered at 5 s or more on HeyGen (4 s on H3, each model's minimum): cut each down to its action beat (times: `take_graph.py --print-prompt`) with `media.py cut <in> <out> 0-<t0> <t1>-<end>` and splice it into its speech take at the scripted point. Optional, only if the user wants it: replace just an action take's audio with ElevenLabs v4 tags (`scripts/voice_replace.py`, `[gulps water]`, `[big tired yawn]`; never onomatopoeia); speech always keeps the model's native audio (better lip-sync). Per ad: `media.py concat` (hook, body, close; loudnorm), then the b-roll with `media.py insert <ad> <out> <clip>@<t0>[+<dur>] ..` (first-person clips over the planned lines; the voice keeps playing; pick clips with `broll.py list --no-product` before the reveal), then lay the real packshot over the planned ranges and add the end card with `media.py overlay <in> <out> <packshot.png> <t0-t1>.. --card=<seconds> [--card-text=<transparent.png>]` (cutaways are silent stills; the take's voice keeps playing; this ffmpeg has no drawtext, so disclosure text comes in as a transparent PNG). Re-review the assembled ad (seams, cutaway label) with `--cutaways <every b-roll and packshot range>` so the planned cuts are not reported. Then `media.py export <ad.mp4> $W/final <name>` produces `_9x16.mp4` (1080x1920) and `_4x5.mp4` (1080x1350, center crop with upward bias so the face stays in frame; check the crop visually). Lay the packshot cutaways and end card after the export, once per format (`overlay` fits the packshot to that file's size; `--pad=white` for a packshot on white), so 4:5 never crops the packshot. HeyGen frames put the head about 3% from the top: the default `--y=0.35` cuts the hair, so pass a lower `--y` (0.1 kept the face whole on 2026-10-01); a product standing low on the counter can't fit with the face in 4:5, so keep the face.
Cut points: splice action takes and trim dead air only inside measured silences (`ffmpeg -af silencedetect=noise=-38dB:d=0.18`), never on model word timestamps (Gemini's drifted up to 2.5 s on 2026-10-01), and check each splice by transcribing the audio on both sides of it.
