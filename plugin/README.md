# Comfy UGC Ads (Claude plugin)

Makes realistic UGC-style video ads from your own brand brief and product packshot: a 3 hooks x 1 body x 3 closes script, keyframe stills, takes with native lip-synced voice on HeyGen Video 1 (MiniMax H3 as the voice-reference fallback), a lint against known video defects, review and assembly, in 9:16 and 4:5.

It bundles the skill `comfy-ugc-ads` and the Comfy Cloud connector (`https://cloud.comfy.org/mcp`). After installing, connect **comfy-cloud** on the plugin's Connectors tab and sign in to your Comfy Cloud account; renders use your Comfy credits. Without the `comfy` CLI (claude.ai chat, Cowork) the skill runs in web mode: `skills/comfy-ugc-ads/references/web-mode.md`.
